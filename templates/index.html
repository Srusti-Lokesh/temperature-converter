Supported conversions

  • °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
  • K = °C + 273.15
  • °C = K − 273.15
  • K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15
  • °F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Input validation prevents temperatures below absolute zero.
